- (requirement by sofia) R6 (DSH.FR-6; parent Constraints NFR-04): Each chart has a keyboard-reachable table view of its numbers and a screen-reader summary naming the period, the total and the largest type.

- (requirement by sofia) R8 (DSH.FR-8, Could; parent R40): Each card shows the absolute and percentage change from the period that ended just before the selected one, marked favourable or unfavourable beyond colour, and shows none for the earliest period, across a gap, or when that period has no activity.
